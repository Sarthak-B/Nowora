You're doing everything right. So why does it feel like something is missing?

The job is fine and the salary is fine. But the alarm goes off and the first thing you feel is a small, heavy sigh. Weekends go too fast and Sunday evenings feel tight.

Nothing is wrong with you. You've been answering "what should I do?" when the more useful question is "what am I for?"

About the author

Meet Yogesh Sharma

Yogesh Sharma writes about purpose, daily habits and the quieter side of ambition. The Ikigai Path grew out of his own search for work that felt worth waking up for, and out of turning Japanese ideas like ikigai, kaizen and wabi-sabi into small steps anyone can take before breakfast.

His approach is simple: no big leaps and no quitting your job on a whim. Just one honest question, one short reading and five minutes of practice a day, repeated until the answer is clear.

I didn't write this book because I had all the answers. I wrote it because I kept asking the same question every morning, and the four circles finally gave me a way to answer it on paper.
