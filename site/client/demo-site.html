01 / The Gur & Grain way

Old recipes. New grains.

Meera started baking for her daughter's tiffin with jowar and jaggery. The neighbours asked for a tray, then a cake, then a wedding. Six years later it's still one kitchen, still small batches.

  • Gur over sugarKolhapuri jaggery gives our bakes a deeper, caramel sweetness, and we use about a third less of it.
  • Millets in the mixRagi, jowar and bajra flours, blended so you get the crumb you love without the heaviness.
  • Baked the morning you get itWe don't keep a display counter. Everything is made for your order.

05 / Around the table

Sweet words. Clean plates.

Before you order.

How early should I order?

Everyday bakes by 6 pm for the next day. Celebration cakes at least 2 days ahead, and wedding orders 2–3 weeks ahead.

Is everything eggless?

Most of our menu can be made eggless at no extra cost. Just mention it when you order.

Do you use any refined sugar?

Our cakes and cookies are sweetened with jaggery. Some icings and decorations need a little refined sugar; we'll tell you which ones.

Where do you deliver?

Across Pune city. Delivery is ₹80–₹180 depending on distance, or pick up free from Lane 6, Koregaon Park.

Can you match a cake design from a photo?

Yes. Send the photo on WhatsApp and we'll sketch our version and give you a fixed price.
